Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Apex Integrated Systems respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why, and what your rights are under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who we are

Apex Integrated Systems is the responsible party for this website. You can contact us at info@apexintegrated.co.za or on 063 211 2172.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you use our quote form, email us, call us or message us on WhatsApp, you may give us your name, phone number, email address, area and the details of your enquiry.
  • Information collected automatically. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Tag Manager and any tools set up inside it may collect information such as your IP address, device and browser type, and the pages you visit.

Our quote form does not store your details on this website. It prepares an email or WhatsApp message that you choose to send to us.

Why we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide quotes.
  • To deliver, install and support the services you ask for.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it, only if you accept analytics.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

Cookies and third parties

  • Google Tag Manager. Loads only after you click Accept. It may set analytics cookies and send data to Google, which may process it outside South Africa.
  • Trustindex. Our reviews widget is provided by Trustindex and may set its own cookies. See their privacy policy for details.
  • Google Fonts. Fonts are loaded from Google, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • WhatsApp and email providers. If you contact us through these, your message is handled under their own privacy terms.

You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business, where the law requires it, or where you have asked us to.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only for as long as we need them to respond to you, provide our services, and meet legal or accounting requirements.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or delete your personal information;
  • stop processing your information or withdraw your consent;

To do any of these, email info@apexintegrated.co.za. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
