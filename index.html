FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what our customers ask most. Can't find yours? Message us on WhatsApp.

What services does Apex Integrated Systems offer?

We install and support CCTV systems, security systems, home automation (including gate and access control), wired networks, wireless networks and Wi-Fi coverage, plus ongoing maintenance and technical support for homes and businesses.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Gauteng's West Rand, including Krugersdorp, Randfontein, Roodepoort, Westonaria, Fochville and Carletonville. If you're nearby, contact us and we'll confirm.

How much does a CCTV installation cost?

It depends on the number of cameras, resolution, cable distances and recording storage. We offer a free, no-obligation site assessment so you get an accurate quote for your property.

How do I get a quote?

Choose a service in the quote form above and email or WhatsApp us, or call 063 211 2172. We'll arrange a site assessment and send a clear quotation.

Can I watch my cameras on my phone?

Yes. We set up secure remote viewing on your smartphone or tablet so you can see live footage and play back recordings from anywhere with an internet connection, and we show you how to use it.

Will my cameras keep working during load shedding?

They can. We connect your recorder, cameras and router to an existing inverter or UPS, or supply a dedicated backup power solution, so your system keeps recording when the grid is down.

Can you repair or upgrade a system installed by someone else?

Yes. We troubleshoot and repair existing systems, add cameras, replace faulty cameras or hard drives, and upgrade older analog setups to HD IP cameras.

Which equipment brands do you work with?

We install and support Dahua, Hikvision, Nemtek, HiLook Reyee and Servility equipment, and can service most major CCTV brands.

Do you help with Wi-Fi and network problems?

Yes. We install structured cabling, mesh Wi-Fi and point-to-point wireless links, and fix dead zones and unstable connections. A stable network also makes your CCTV and remote viewing more reliable.

What can home automation do for me?

Home automation lets you control things like your garage door or gate, lighting and access from your phone or on a schedule. We design a setup around how you live.

Do you offer support after installation?

Yes. We make sure you know how to use your system, review footage and adjust settings, and we offer maintenance and fault-finding if something goes wrong later.

What are your working hours?

We're available Monday to Sunday, 08:00 to 18:00.
